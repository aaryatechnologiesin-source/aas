FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CLEAR ANSWERS

How long does a website take? +
Typically, standard informational sites take 1–2 weeks, while larger custom web applications or complex Shopify builds take 2–4 weeks depending on specific scope.
Do you provide Shopify development? +
Yes, we engineer custom Shopify storefronts optimized specifically for fast page loads, high conversion rates, and seamless inventory management.
Is SEO included? +
Every project includes fundamental technical SEO setup: clean semantic structure, meta-tag mapping, fast page speed optimization, and search engine indexing.
Can you automate my business? +
Yes, we build intelligent workflows connecting your website to tools like WhatsApp, email platforms, CRMs, and payment gateways to automate lead handling.
Do you provide domain and hosting? +
We assist you in selecting and configuring high-performance hosting services and registering domains directly under your ownership.
What does lifetime support mean? +
We guarantee continuous technical assistance for bug fixes, code upkeep, and operational guidance long after your platform goes live.
